## Homepage sequence

1. One-sentence positioning and human summary.
2. Compact proof band: 20+ years, EY Senior Manager, Tallan VP and
   100+ engagements only when the final resume wording supports it.
3. Featured work: CapHub, Proskauer and ThriftTrack.
4. How Ben works: discovery/service design → architecture/delivery → adoption
   and continuous improvement.
5. Nuology as current building energy, separated from client work.
6. Writing/speaking credibility and a simple contact CTA.

## Initial project priorities

### First public case-study work

- **CapHub:** lead product person / overall Product Owner for a multi-module
  public-sector housing transformation. Confirm public scope and outcomes.
- **Proskauer:** legal-sector Power BI dashboard and client-intelligence story.
  Confirm public-safe scope, dates, visuals and contribution detail.
- **Power BI cluster:** Brixmor, Proskauer and Montefiore as evidence of
  repeatable analytics, reporting and UX capability.
- **ThriftTrack:** launched Nuology product story. Confirm responsibilities,
  launch date, visuals and approved outcomes.

### Protected or later

- **CSAA NextGen** and **WME Pulse** were important, challenging engagements.
  Preserve them for the approved full view or later carefully reviewed stories;
  do not turn their difficulties into unsupported public claims.
- HireRight, Revolv3, Gov2Go, Brixmor, Montefiore, CHIA, AccessMaps, Cotiviti,
  Experian, XL, YoungWilliams and Broadridge need additional fact, sharing or
  outcome confirmation before becoming public case studies.
- Other Nuology products remain status- and shareability-dependent. Do not
  describe them as launched.
