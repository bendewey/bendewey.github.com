# Content plan

## Positioning

**Working headline:** Technology transformation and product delivery leader for
complex digital platforms.

Lead with the business and delivery problems Ben solves. Support that story with
roles, selected project narratives and meaningful technical depth—not a
technology laundry list.

Ben is now independent, based in Charleston, South Carolina, and seeking his
next leadership opportunity. He is also available for select advisory work.
His earlier EY leadership work belongs in the career narrative, not in the
site’s present-tense positioning. Earlier writing, talks and open-source
work provide supporting evidence of technical depth and community contribution.
Use curated, dated selections rather than a homepage that depends on recent
posts or an active event calendar. No new publishing schedule is required.

## Homepage sequence

1. One-sentence positioning and human summary.
2. Compact proof band: relevant experience, selected leadership roles, author/
   speaker/community evidence, and current availability only when the final
   résumé wording supports it.
3. Featured work: a public-sector housing department, a top NYC law firm, and
   ThriftTrack.
4. How Ben works: discovery/service design → architecture/delivery → adoption
   and continuous improvement.
5. Nuology as founder work, separated from the current job-search narrative.
6. Writing/speaking credibility and a simple contact CTA.

## Legacy migration

Ben has authorized use of his own published blog content at
[bendewey.wordpress.com](https://bendewey.wordpress.com/). The visible archive
includes 2008–2010 material and a February 21, 2010 announcement moving readers
to BenDewey.com. This supplements the later self-hosted WordPress recovery.
Keep original dates and source URLs; reconcile duplicate posts and verify
linked slides/code before making them available as downloads.

| Material | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| 2010–2015 WordPress posts | Archive under writing with visible dates; promote only still-useful pieces. |
| Old bio/contact pages | Replace and redirect. |
| Samples, slides and downloads | Audit licensing, credentials and dead dependencies before indexing. |
| Old resumes | Replace with one current web/PDF resume; keep old files private unless needed. |
| Historical subapps | Do not deploy by default; evaluate only as historical artifacts. |

## Contact

The site will offer a short contact form (name, email, company/role, and
message) alongside LinkedIn and a downloadable résumé. The form sends an email
to Ben through a configured transactional-email provider; its recipient and
provider credentials stay in Azure App Service settings. Add a honeypot and
rate limiting before launch.
